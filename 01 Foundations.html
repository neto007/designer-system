Do
  • Use one neon per component to anchor its role.
  • Pair every neon with a glow shadow at ~35 % alpha.
  • Reserve ds-red strictly for destructive and error states.
Don't
  • Mix three or more neons inside a single card.
  • Use neon as a body-text background fill.
  • Tint surfaces above ds-selection. We stay near black.
