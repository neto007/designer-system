Principles

five rules that shape every decision
01

Terminal_first

Power users live in CLIs. Mirror their syntax: snake_case, monospace ids, prefixed verbs.

02

Color_is_meaning

Every neon owns one concept. Never paint with palette — encode with it.

03

Glow_signals_state

Glow = live. Hard offset shadow = tactile. Both replace gradients and soft drop‑shadows.

04

Density_with_dignity

High information density without crushing — tight type, wide tracking, ample dark space.

05

Voice_is_a_log

Speak like a system writing to stdout. No fluff, no exclamations, no emoji. Precision > politeness.
